Driver or CX?

Type the collection and delivery postcodes. Compares the NW driver rate card (per loaded mile, minimum charge) against the engine's expected CX cost for every vehicle, and tells you which usually makes more sense.
Miles are estimated from area centroids (straight line × 1.25) unless you type the real road miles.

CX = the engine's expected third-party cost for this lane (pure CX cost base). Some drivers sit above the card, so treat wins under 10% as a coin toss. Availability still rules: a driver already positioned beats both numbers.
